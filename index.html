Who we are

Building from the foundation to the sky

Avenue Group has helped shape concrete forming into the dynamic industry it is today, with a portfolio of landmark projects spanning more than six decades.

We specialize in the niche that others avoid: architecturally exposed concrete, complex geometry and time-sensitive schedules. Developers, construction managers and general contractors return to us project after project because we deliver on time and on budget.

  • Architecturally exposed and complex concrete designs
  • Commercial, institutional and residential expertise
  • Long-standing relationships with major builders and developers
