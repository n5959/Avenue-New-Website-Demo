Accessibility Standards for Ontarians with Disabilities Policy

Our commitment to accessible customer service and employment under the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005 (AODA).

Purpose

This policy addresses the requirements of Ontario Regulation 429/07, Accessibility Standards for Customer Service, made under the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005.

Policy statement

Avenue Building Corporation is committed to eliminating barriers and improving accessibility for persons with disabilities in a way that respects dignity, independence, integration and equal opportunity. We recognise the diverse needs of all people and strive to provide services and facilities that are accessible to everyone.

Principles

  • People with disabilities have an equal opportunity to obtain, use and benefit from our goods and services.
  • Goods and services are provided in a way that respects each person’s independence and dignity.
  • Goods and services are integrated, unless an alternative measure (temporary or permanent) is needed for a person to fully benefit.
  • People with disabilities may use assistive devices, service animals and support persons as needed for access.

Scope

This policy applies to management, office staff and all other employees of Avenue Building Corporation at 50 Nixon Road, Bolton, Ontario.

Definitions

Assistive devices

Aids used to replace, compensate for or improve the functional abilities of people with disabilities — for example mobility and visual aids, prosthetics, speech devices, medical supplies, environmental controls and respiratory devices.

Barrier

Anything that prevents a person with a disability from fully participating because of their disability.

Disability

As defined in the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005 and the Ontario Human Rights Code, including physical disabilities, mental impairments, developmental and learning disabilities, mental disorders, and injuries or disabilities for which benefits were claimed or received under the Workplace Safety and Insurance Act, 1997.

Procedures

Assistive devices

People with disabilities may use their assistive devices to obtain and use our goods and services unless prohibited for health, safety or privacy reasons. The person is responsible for operating their device safely.

Communication

We communicate with people with disabilities in ways that take their disability into account, and we communicate our accessibility policies and procedures to staff through office meetings and training.

Service animals

People with disabilities may be accompanied by their service animal in areas of our premises that are open to the public.

Support persons

People with disabilities may be accompanied by a support person in areas open to the public, and will not be prevented from having access to their support person. Consent is required before confidential information is discussed in the support person’s presence. A support person may be required for health and safety reasons only after consultation with the person with a disability, and only when it is the only way to allow access.

Feedback process

Feedback about the goods and services we provide can be given in person, by mail, email, telephone or fax. A copy of the feedback process and form is available on request. If you provide contact information, we will make an effort to respond within two business days.

Service disruptions

If a planned disruption affects facilities, services or systems relied on to access Avenue Building Corporation, notice will include the reason for the disruption and any alternative measures available. Notice of unexpected disruptions will be given as soon as possible.

Training

Employees receive training on providing goods and services to people with disabilities as soon as practicable after being assigned applicable duties, and on an ongoing basis as policies change. Training covers:

  • The Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005 and Ontario Regulation 429/07
  • How to interact and communicate with people with various types of disabilities
  • How to interact with people who use assistive devices, service animals or support persons
  • What to do if a person with a disability is having difficulty accessing our services

Responsibilities

Avenue Building Corporation is responsible for ensuring compliance with Ontario Regulation 429/07, providing and recording accessible customer service training, communicating these policies to all employees, and posting this policy at our head office at 50 Nixon Road, Bolton.

Multi-Year Accessibility Plan

Our Multi-Year Accessibility Plan (original date April 19, 2017; revised May 2017) sets out how we identify, prevent and remove barriers under the Integrated Accessibility Standards Regulation (IASR, Ontario Regulation 191/11). We review and update the plan at least once every five years and prepare accessibility status reports at least every two years.

Statement of commitment

  • We are committed to treating all people in a way that allows them to maintain their dignity and independence.
  • We believe in integration and equal opportunity.
  • We are committed to meeting the needs of people with disabilities in a timely manner by identifying, preventing and removing barriers and meeting the requirements of the AODA.

Plan highlights

  • Emergency information (2013): emergency procedures reviewed for the needs of people with disabilities; individualized workplace emergency response plans for employees who need them.
  • Policies, plan, procurement and training (2014): accessibility policies updated for IASR requirements; accessibility criteria included when procuring goods, services and facilities; ongoing AODA training for all employees.
  • Information and communications (2014): accessible feedback processes and accessible documents and web content.
  • Employment (2014): accommodation available to applicants during recruitment, documented individual accommodation plans, and a return-to-work process for employees absent due to a disability.
  • Accessible formats (2015): accessible formats and communication supports provided on request, in a timely manner.
  • Websites (2021): websites and web content to conform to WCAG 2.0 Level AA.

Avenue Building Corporation does not provide transportation services as described in the IASR Transportation Standard, so that section does not apply.
