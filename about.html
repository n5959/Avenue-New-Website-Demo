About Us

We are a leading concrete forming contractor for architecturally exposed and complex concrete in Ontario.

Our story

Building from the foundation to the sky

Founded in 1965, Avenue Building Corporation has played a major part in shaping concrete forming into the dynamic industry it is today.

Our portfolio includes many of Ontario’s landmark buildings: museums and concert halls, hospitals and transit stations, head offices and some of the tallest residential towers in Toronto.

With a solid reputation in the concrete forming business Avenue Group has built strong relationships with every major developer, construction manager, and general contractor, which have entrusted Avenue Group to make their project a success on an ongoing basis.

Milestones

Six decades of landmarks

Selected highlights from our project retrospective.

  1. 1965

    Avenue Group is founded

    The company begins its long history in the concrete forming business.

  2. 1980

    Esso Plaza Office Buildings

    The earliest project in our retrospective, at 3,500,000 sq ft.

  3. 1990

    Canadian Broadcasting Centre

    Headquarters building in downtown Toronto — 3,000,000 sq ft.

  4. 1998

    Air Canada Centre

    Concrete forming for Toronto's downtown arena — 800,000 sq ft.

  5. 2004

    Royal Ontario Museum Crystal

    The Renaissance ROM Crystal — exposed, angular concrete at its most demanding.

  6. 2007

    Maple Leaf Square

    One of our largest single projects at 3,785,000 sq ft.

  7. 2012

    Humber River Regional Hospital

    A 3,104,000 sq ft acute-care hospital in Toronto.

  8. 2018

    The Well

    Buildings A–F delivered through the Avenue-Verdi joint venture — 8,000,000 sq ft.

  9. 2024

    481 University Avenue

    Our most recent listed project — 2,300,000 sq ft in downtown Toronto.
